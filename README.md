Computer Science senior at UConn (graduating May 2027), and a U.S. Air Force veteran (seven years in Security Forces before I started coding). I like building things that people will actually use, and I care about making them reliable. 

## What I'm building

**[Roll Credits](https://github.com/blynch174/roll-credits-app-project)**: a phone-based movie marathon app that picks what to watch with dice rolls. Inspired by my combined love of Dungeons & Dragons and Halloween movie marathons, I started making a handwritten movie menu that I would roll my D&D dice on for my annual October 'festival'. That grew into the app it is today and using Firebase for user accounts and a multi-device sync. The app is currently being tested for feedback and improvements. 

**[Turbo Ducks Telemtry](https://github.com/blynch174/sdp-team20-td)** *(senior capstone)*: a live telemetry dashboard and anomaly detection/prediction system for UConn's Formula SAE race car, sponsored by OWL Integrations. I manage a 4-person team and work on the dashboard and split the work on the edge pipeline (RaspPi -> LoRa -> Pit dashboard). Currently still under construction.

**[BackBeat](https://github.com/blynch174/backbeat-program)** *(in progress)*: drum hit classification and transcription using the IDMT-SMT-Drums and Expanded Groove MIDI datasets. Started as a big data analytics project getting the model running, and will continue to develop when the semester ends.
